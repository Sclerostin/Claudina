AMD Day Trader

It waits for the morning shakeout. A stock builds its opening box, dips under it to take the stops, then closes back above it. On market fear days that pattern earned +0.45R a trade in the holdout test. It buys, aims for 2R, and is out by 3:55 PM.

Your morning, in order

  1. 9:25 AM

    Read the day grade

    Open AMD Radar on SPY's 5-minute chart. The top row says Day A, B or X. Only A days are traded. That's about two days a month.

  2. 9:30 – 9:45

    The box forms

    The first 15 minutes set each stock's opening range. Nothing to do.

  3. 9:45 – 11:00

    Watch for the shakeout

    A phone message says a stock dipped under its box or yesterday's low. It now has a BUY line: the top of its box. Open that chart.

  4. by 12:00

    BUY on the strong close

    A green 5-minute candle closes above the box, near its high. The bridge buys for you, or you place the bracket order yourself in a few taps.

  5. until 3:55 PM

    Hands off

    The stop waits at moomoo and the target is 2R. Whatever is still open is sold at 3:55. One setup per stock per day, so there's no re-entry to chase.

Accumulation · Manipulation · Distribution

The setup

Illustrative 5-minute chart, not real prices. Box from 9:30 to 9:45, the dip under it at 10:05, BUY at 10:30, target reached at 11:20.

A

Accumulation

The first 15 minutes build a box. Orders pile up just under its low and under yesterday's low.

M

Manipulation

Before 11:00, price dips under the box or yesterday's low and takes those stops. Wyckoff called it a spring; ICT calls it the Judas swing.

D

Distribution

Before 12:00, a green candle closes above the box high, in the top 40% of its range. That close is the BUY.

Stop

Just under the lowest point of the dip, but never more than 2 five-minute ATRs below the entry.

Target and exit

2× the risk. If neither is hit, the position is sold at 3:55 PM. The average trade lasted about 3 hours.

Market check

No entry while SPY is below its VWAP. The setup only counts once per stock per day.

Size

Shares = your risk per trade ÷ (entry − stop). At $25,000 and 0.5% that's $125 lost on a stop-out.

Day grades

The same pattern behaves very differently depending on the market's mood that morning. AMD Day Trader reads SPY's daily chart before the open and grades the day.

GradeWhenWhat happens
AFear daySPY's RSI(2) closed at 10 or lower yesterday, or SPY opens at least 1 daily ATR below yesterday's closeSignals are traded
BNormalEverything elseGrey B marks on the chart, not traded
XEuphoriaSPY opens at least 1 daily ATR above yesterday's closeNo longs at all

Why: across 11 years of daily data, stocks drifted up from the open after the market was oversold or gapped down hard, and drifted down after it gapped up hard. Connors' RSI(2) work and Wyckoff's selling climax describe the same thing.

Read before you trade it

This is a strong lead, not a proven edge. It rests on 16 days, half the trades came from March's sell-off, and the grade rule was found after the first holdout test failed. It still has to pass a fresh test: yours, on years of data (see Verify), then a month of paper trading.

What failed

Afternoon versions, re-entries after the first trade, 5-minute swing sweeps, VWAP-band springs, time stops, trend and relative-volume filters all lost money or added nothing. In August and September, SPY's gains came overnight while most sessions closed below their open, and every long day-trade idea lost money then. The full write-up is in research/RESULTS.md.

Install

  1. Copy AMD Day Trader

    Use the Copy button below. In TradingView open the Pine Editor, create a new strategy, delete everything in it, paste, and save. Line 1 must read //@version=6.

  2. Add it to a 5-minute chart

    Regular trading hours only, which is TradingView's default for stocks. Open the settings, then Risk: enter your account size and risk per trade. Under Properties, set Initial capital to the same amount.

  3. Add AMD Radar to SPY

    Create a new indicator the same way and paste AMD Radar. Put it on SPY's 5-minute chart and list your 12 stocks in its settings.

  4. Optional: DT Volume

    Volume bars that light up at 1.5× the average. Same steps.

// AMD Day Trader - morning Accumulation / Manipulation / Distribution buys, graded by the market's day
//
// THE SETUP (tested on 47 US stocks, 5-minute candles, Feb 23 - Oct 2 2026)
//   A  Accumulation : the opening range, the first 15 minutes (the "box").
//   M  Manipulation : before 11:00 AM price dips BELOW the box low or yesterday's low and takes
//                     the stops resting there (Wyckoff spring, ICT "Judas swing").
//   D  Distribution : before 12:00 PM a strong green candle closes back ABOVE the box high.
//                     BUY at that close. Stop under the manipulation low (never more than 2 ATR).
//                     Target 2R. Anything still open is sold at 3:55 PM. One setup per stock per day.
//
// GRADES - the market's day, read from SPY's daily chart before the open
//   A  fear day : SPY's RSI(2) closed at 10 or lower yesterday, or SPY opened 1 daily ATR or more
//                 lower. Grade A was profitable in both test periods (+0.49R and +0.45R a trade).
//   B  normal   : the same pattern lost money from July to October 2026. Paper-test only.
//   X  SPY opened 1 daily ATR or more higher: stocks tend to drift down all day. No signals.
//
// Webhook messages are JSON for the AMD moomoo bridge. Educational tool, not financial advice.

float riskPx = close - stopPx
bool  riskOk = riskPx >= 0.3 * atr

bool  sigB   = trigger and riskOk and mktOk and grade == "B"
bool  take   = (sigA or (sigB and takeWhat == "A and B")) and strategy.position_size == 0 and not eodBar

    math.max(1.0, math.floor(math.min(byRisk, byCash)))

// AMD state of one stock, computed on that stock's own 5-minute candles.
// state: 0 closed, 1 accumulation, 2 waiting for manipulation, 3 manipulation done,
//        4 BUY given, 5 no setup today
// result: 0 none, 1 open, 2 target, 3 stop, 4 closed at 3:55
amd(int orLen, float cap, float tR) =>
    string NYz = "America/New_York"
    int  m     = hour(time, NYz) * 60 + minute(time, NYz)
    int  d     = year(time, NYz) * 10000 + month(time, NYz) * 100 + dayofmonth(time, NYz)
    bool rth   = m >= 570 and m < 960
    float atr  = ta.atr(14)
    var int   cur      = na
    var float prevLow  = na
    var float dayLow   = na
    var float orH      = na
    var float orL      = na
    var bool  swept    = false
    var float mLow     = na
    var bool  used     = false
    var bool  bought   = false
    var float ePx      = na
    var float sPx      = na
    var float tPx      = na
    var int   bTime    = na
    var int   result   = 0
    bool start = rth and (na(cur) or d != cur)
    if start
        prevLow := dayLow
        dayLow  := low
        cur     := d
        orH     := high
        orL     := low
        swept   := false
        mLow    := na
        used    := false
        bought  := false
        result  := 0
    else if rth
        dayLow := math.min(dayLow, low)
        if m < 570 + orLen
            orH := math.max(orH, high)
            orL := math.min(orL, low)
    bool  afterOR = rth and m >= 570 + orLen
    float line    = na(prevLow) ? orL : math.max(orL, prevLow)
    bool  sweep   = afterOR and m < 660 and not swept and not na(orL) and low < line
    if sweep
        swept := true
        mLow  := low
    else if swept and not used
        mLow := math.min(mLow, low)
    // a trade from earlier today: stop first if one candle touches both
    if bought and result == 1
        if low <= sPx
            result := 3
        else if high >= tPx
            result := 2
        else if m + 5 >= 955
            result := 4
    float rng  = high - low
    bool  trig = afterOR and m < 720 and swept and not used and close > open and rng > 0 and (close - low) >= 0.6 * rng and close > orH and close[1] <= orH
    float stp  = math.max(mLow, close - cap * atr) - 0.1 * atr
    bool  buy  = trig and close - stp >= 0.3 * atr and m + 5 < 955
    if trig
        used := true
    if buy
        bought := true
        ePx    := close
        sPx    := stp
        tPx    := close + tR * (close - stp)
        bTime  := m
        result := 1
    int state = not rth ? 0 : m < 570 + orLen ? 1 : bought ? 4 : used ? 5 : not swept and m < 660 ? 2 : not swept ? 5 : m < 720 ? 3 : 5
    [state, line, orH, mLow, sweep, buy, ePx, sPx, tPx, bTime, result]

Execute

By hand, from the alert

  1. Tap the BUY notification. It opens the chart and shows shares, stop and target.

  2. Long-press the chart and tap + to create an order with your connected moomoo account.

  3. Buy, Limit, at the alert price. Enter the shares.

  4. Turn on Take profit (the target) and Stop loss (the stop). Place it.

  5. At 3:55 PM, close anything still open.

Automatically, with the bridge

A small program on a computer that stays on receives the Orders alerts and trades your moomoo account through moomoo OpenAPI.

  • Limit buy, never chases more than 0.15%.
  • The stop is placed at moomoo the moment shares fill.
  • Sells everything at 3:55 PM by its own clock.
  • Grade A only, 3 positions at most, stops for the day at −3R.
  • Pause it from your phone's browser.

Run it in dry run for a few days, paper for a month, then live small. Setup steps: moomoo_bridge/README.md.

Verify on years of data

This is where your paid plan pays for itself. The study had 7 months of 5-minute data; TradingView has years. Check grade A before you trust it.

  1. Open a stock's 5-minute chart with AMD Day Trader. Keep "Trade which signals" on A only.

  2. Open the Strategy Tester, turn on Deep Backtesting, set the start to 2018 or the earliest available, and generate the report. Bar Magnifier is already on in the script, so stops and targets fill in the right order.

  3. Write down Total closed trades and Avg trade. Average R = Avg trade ÷ your risk per trade (for example $40 ÷ $125 = +0.32R).

  4. Repeat on 10 to 20 of your stocks and add them up. Grade A should stay clearly positive across years and most stocks. Switch to A and B to see how normal days compare.

  5. Use Bar Replay on a few A days to practise the routine at real speed before going live.

Read the chart

Blue band: the box. Its top line is the BUY line.

Yellow line: yesterday's low, the other stop pool under the market.

▲ M

M: the dip. From here, red dots track the lowest point until the BUY or 12:00.

BUY A

BUY: entry at that candle's close. Red line is the stop, green line the target, grey the entry.

● B

B: the same pattern on a normal day. Not traded; useful for paper tracking.

Green tint: grade A day. Red tint: grade X day.

The panel shows Today (the grade), Why (SPY's RSI(2), gap and VWAP), Phase (where the stock is: box forming, waiting for the dip, waiting for the close above the box, or done), Plan, and the grade A and B results on the chart's history.
