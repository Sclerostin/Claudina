What DT Core 4 trades

The VWAP spring

Wyckoff called it a spring, Linda Raschke called it Turtle Soup, and ICT calls it the manipulation leg. Price is pushed below an obvious support to trip stops, then buyers take it straight back. DT Core 4 trades that shakeout at the lower VWAP band, a statistical extreme for the day, and only on stocks with real volume behind them.

  1. The stock is in play. Volume since the open is at least 1.5× normal for this time of day.
  2. A candle dips below the lower VWAP band (2 standard deviations below VWAP) and closes back above it.
  3. The candle is green and closes near its high, in the top 40% of its range. Buyers won the candle.
  4. BUY at the close. The stop goes just under the candle's low; the target is 2× the risk.
  5. Out in about an hour on average, at the target, the stop or 3:55 PM. Then it looks for the next spring. Up to 3 trades per stock per day, entries 9:45 AM to 3:00 PM, and it stops for the day after losing 2R.

Why the opening range breakout is gone. I backtested DT Core 3's breakout rules on four months of real data and they lost money: −0.17R per trade in the test period and −0.20R afterwards. It's still in the settings as an experimental option, off by default.

Backtest on real data

What the testing showed

The test data was 5-minute candles for 20 liquid stocks and ETFs (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, SPY, QQQ and others) from June 1 to October 2, 2026, or 87 trading days. Each candle was replayed the way TradingView runs the strategy, with slippage. Rules were chosen on June–August, then checked on September onward.

Accumulation · manipulation · distribution

The phase monitor

The panel's Phase row labels what the order flow looks like right now. It uses 1-minute volume delta (buying volume minus selling volume), which your paid plan provides through lower-timeframe data. It's a reading aid: it wasn't part of the test, so it doesn't trigger trades.

PhaseWhat the monitor seesWhat it means for you
ACCUMULATIONPrice near VWAP, volume delta rising for 30 minutesBuyers are absorbing supply. Springs from here are the best ones.
MANIPULATION ↓ springIn the last 3 candles, price broke below the lower band, the opening range low or yesterday's low, then closed back aboveThe shakeout. If the stock is in play and the candle qualifies, DT Core buys. Otherwise a grey diamond marks it.
EXPANSION UPAbove VWAP + 1σ, 9 EMA over 20 EMAThe move after accumulation (ICT calls this "distribution"). Let open trades work; don't chase.
DISTRIBUTIONPrice at the day's high, volume delta fallingWyckoff's distribution: sellers unloading into strength. Expect a pullback.
MANIPULATION ↑ upthrustA spike above the upper band that closes back below it on a red candle (red diamond)A bull trap. Don't buy here.
EXPANSION DOWNBelow VWAP − 1σ, 9 EMA under 20 EMASellers in control. Wait for a spring at the lower band.
BALANCENone of the aboveNo edge. Wait.

Turn on "Phone alert when the phase changes" in settings to get a ping on each change.

Continuous execution

Daily routine

More trades come from watching more in-play stocks, not from loosening the rules. A watchlist of 5 to 10 names with DT Core on each gives you several chances a day.

  1. 9:00 AM: build today's list in TradingView's Stock Screener. Filters: Relative Volume ≥ 1.5, Pre-market Change ≥ 2% (or ≤ −2%), Price ≥ $5, Average Volume (10 day) ≥ 1M. Add a catalyst check: news or earnings.
  2. Save the top 5–10 as a watchlist named "In play today".
  3. Open each on a 5-minute chart with DT Core 4 and create one alert per stock: alert() calls for phone pings, or Order fills → webhook for the moomoo bridge.
  4. Trade the signals, nothing else. The panel's Today row tracks trades and R. DT Core stops itself after 3 trades or −2R on each stock.
  5. After the close, delete today's alerts and review the BUY and EXIT labels. Tomorrow's list will be different.
  • WebhooksSend every fill to the moomoo bridge for hands-off orders.
  • Many active alertsOne per in-play stock, every day.
  • Intrabar data1-minute volume delta for the phase monitor.
  • Multi-chart layoutsWatch several in-play stocks at once on iPad or desktop.
  • Deep Backtesting, Bar ReplayTest DT Core on a new stock before trading it, and practice by replaying past sessions candle by candle.

Three ways to execute with moomoo

moomoo is an official TradingView broker for US stocks, so you can trade straight from the chart.

1
Tap to trade on the TradingView chart

Connect moomoo once in the chart's trading panel. When BUY SPRING prints, tap Buy → Market, enter the share count from the Size row, turn on Take profit and Stop loss with the prices from the Plan row, and confirm. moomoo receives it as one bracket order.

2
Get pinged, then use the moomoo app

The phone alert reads "BUY NVDA (SPRING) @ 98.25 - stop 97.82 - target 99.11 - 290 shares". In moomoo: Trade → Pro mode → Attached orders → Bracket.

3
Fully automatic with the DT Core bridge

A small program on a computer that stays on during market hours places every DT Core trade in your moomoo account through moomoo's official OpenAPI, with a protective stop. It handles several trades a day per stock. Setup is in tradingview/moomoo_bridge/README.md; start in dry-run, then paper.

Alerts

  1. Phone (levels 1 and 2): on the stock's 5-minute chart, add an alert with Condition DT Core → alert() function calls only, app push and sound on.
  2. moomoo bridge (level 3): Condition DT Core → Order fills only, Message exactly {{strategy.order.alert_message}}, and your bridge URL under Webhook URL.

Reading the chart

  • BUY SPRINGAll the spring rules passed on this candle. Entry is its closing price.
  • Stop, just under the spring candle's low.
  • Target, 2× the risk above entry.
  • TARGET +2.0RTarget hit. +2R on $125 risk is +$250.
  • STOP -1.0RStop hit: a planned, limited loss.
  • EXITClosed at 3:55 PM without reaching either.
  • VWAP: green when price is above it, red below.
  • Lower band, the spring line. Thicker orange line 2 standard deviations below VWAP. The faint one above is the upper band.
  • ◆ greyA spring that wasn't a trade: the stock isn't in play, the candle is weak, or it's outside trading hours.
  • ◆ redUpthrust above the upper band. A warning, not a trade.
  • Opening range (first 15 minutes). Yellow lines are yesterday's high and low.
  • Midday shading, 2 to 4 hours after the open.

Earlier questions

I saved the script. Why is nothing on the chart?

Saving and adding are two separate steps, and you need both. Saving stores the code in your TradingView account; if it saved without a red error message, the code compiled. Adding puts it on a chart:

On the chart, tap ƒx Indicators → Personal (some versions say My scripts) → tap DT Core. Its name appears in the top-left corner of the chart.

Added but still blank?

  • Daily chart. DT Core only draws on intraday charts. Switch to 5 minutes; the panel says so if you forget.
  • Indicator limit. The free plan fits very few indicators per chart. Remove Volume or anything else you're not using (tap its name → remove).
  • Hidden. Tap DT Core's name at the top left and make sure the eye icon is on.
  • Before the open or on a weekend. You'll see earlier days' lines and signals; today's start at 9:30.

What do "0930-1000" and "0930-0935" mean?

They were time windows on a 24-hour clock in New York time, written start-end without colons. DT Core no longer asks for them: you pick Opening range length: Auto, 5, 15 or 30 minutes, and it measures from the opening bell on its own.

The chart settings didn't match my app.

Menus differ between app versions, and most of those settings didn't affect the signals. DT Core handles the timezone inside the script. The only chart settings you need: Candles, the 5-minute timeframe, and ETH on if you want pre-market lines.

To reach chart settings for anything else, long-press the price numbers on the right edge of the chart, then tap Settings.

DT Momentum and DT Volume

Optional extras

DT Core's signals don't depend on either one. Your plan has plenty of indicator room, so it comes down to what helps you read the chart.

  • DT Volume: keepIts bright bars now light up at 1.5× average volume, the same test DT Core uses. A BUY or SELL candle always has a bright volume bar under it, so you can see why the signal fired. Paste the new version below over your saved copy.
