Get it running

About "Script could not be translated from: |B|W|E|". TradingView shows this when it can't read the script's first line, //@version=6, or when its translator chokes on an unusual construct. DT Core 3 is rewritten in plain ASCII text using only long-established Pine functions. Paste it into an empty editor so nothing old is left above line 1.

  1. Copy DT Core 3 with the button below.
  2. Empty the editor completely. Pine Editor → open DT Core → Select All → Delete. Scroll up and down: the editor must be blank.
  3. Paste and check line 1. It must read exactly //@version=6, with nothing before it on the line. Then Save.
  4. Re-add it to the chart. DT Core is now a strategy, so remove the old copy from the chart (tap its name → remove), then ƒx Indicators → Personal → DT Core.
  5. 5-minute candles, then your numbers. Tap DT Core → Settings → Inputs → Risk: account size and risk per trade. Under Properties, set Initial capital to the same account size.

Open chart → see signal → execute · moomoo

Three ways to execute with moomoo

moomoo is an official TradingView broker for US stocks and options, so you can trade straight from the chart. Each level builds on the last.

1
Tap to trade on the TradingView chart

Connect once: open the chart's trading panel, choose moomoo, and log in. When BUY prints, tap Buy → Market, enter the share count from the panel's Size row, turn on Take profit and Stop loss with the prices from the Plan row, and confirm. moomoo receives it as one bracket order. About 15 seconds.

2
Get pinged, then use the moomoo app

Add the plain-language alert below. When it arrives, open the stock in moomoo → Trade → Pro mode → Attached orders → Bracket, and enter the share count, take-profit and stop-loss from the alert.

3
Fully automatic with the DT Core bridge

Automation services like TradersPost don't support moomoo, so this kit includes its own bridge. It's a small program on a computer that's on during market hours. DT Core sends each order to the bridge through a webhook, and the bridge places it in your moomoo account through moomoo's official OpenAPI, with a protective stop. Nothing to tap.

Alert for levels 1 and 2

  1. Open the stock's 5-minute chart. DT Core must be on it.
  2. Add an alert. Tap the alarm-clock button, or long-press the chart → Add alert.
  3. Condition: DT Core → alert() function calls only.
  4. Notify: app push and sound on. Create.
BUY NVDA @ 102.00 - stop 100.75 - target 104.50 - 100 shares (risk $125) EXIT NVDA @ 104.50 - TARGET +2.0R

Level 3: the moomoo bridge

It needs a Mac, Windows or Linux computer that stays awake from 9:25 AM to 4:05 PM New York time, or a small cloud server. Full instructions are in tradingview/moomoo_bridge/README.md on your GitHub branch.

  1. On the computer: install moomoo's OpenD gateway and log in, install Python and moomoo-api, then start bridge.py.
  2. Give it an HTTPS address with a free ngrok static domain. TradingView only sends webhooks over HTTPS.
  3. In TradingView, on each stock's 5-minute chart: add an alert with Condition DT Core → Order fills only, Message exactly {{strategy.order.alert_message}}, and your bridge URL under Webhook URL.
  4. Roll out in three stages: dry run (logs only) → moomoo paper account → live with small size.

What DT Core sends, and what the bridge does with it:

{"ticker":"NVDA","action":"buy","quantity":100,"price":102.00,"takeProfit":{"limitPrice":104.50},"stopLoss":{"type":"stop","stopPrice":100.75}} → market BUY US.NVDA x100, then stop SELL x100 at 100.75 {"ticker":"NVDA","action":"exit","price":104.50} → cancel US.NVDA open orders, market SELL x100
  • Safety limitsThe bridge refuses orders above your max shares or max dollar value, tickers outside your list, shorts if you turn them off, and a second entry while you already hold the stock.
  • Keep it awakeIf the computer sleeps, entries and exits are missed. The stop at moomoo still protects you, but it is a day order, so check moomoo before the close on any day you're unsure.
  • Settings freezeAn alert keeps the DT Core settings it was made with. Change a setting, then delete and recreate the alert.
  • Not a guaranteeReal fills differ from simulated ones. Start in paper mode and keep the risk per trade small.

Reading the signals

Labels appear when a candle closes and never move or disappear afterward.

  • BUYA candle closed above the opening range, above VWAP, on strong volume. Entry is that candle's closing price.
  • SELLThe mirror image below the range. SELL means sell short. If you only buy stocks, turn it off in settings.
  • Stop. Where the trade is wrong.
  • Target. Twice as far from entry as the stop, so a win pays 2× what a loss costs.
  • Entry price.
  • TARGET +2.0RTarget hit. R means "times your risk": +2R on $125 risk is +$250.
  • STOP -1.0RStop hit: a planned, limited loss.
  • EXITNeither was hit, so the trade closed 5 minutes before the market close.
  • VWAP, the day's volume-weighted average price. Green when price is above it, red when below.
  • Opening range. Breakouts from this box are the signals.
  • Yesterday's high and low.
  • Pre-market high and low. Tap ETH at the bottom right of the chart to show extended hours; futures show the overnight range automatically.
  • Midday, 2 to 4 hours after the open. Breakouts fail more often here.

TradingView also draws its own small order arrows for strategies. To hide them, open DT Core's settings → Style and turn off the trades-on-chart option.

Status moves through the day like this:

Waiting for the open→Opening range forming→Watching until 11:30 AM→IN A BUY / SELL TRADE→No new signals today
  • PlanWhile watching, it shows the exact trigger prices ("BUY above 101.60 · SELL below 99.90"). In a trade, it shows entry, stop and target.
  • SizeShares to buy so that hitting the stop loses exactly your risk per trade (Tharp's position sizing). "≈" means an estimate before the trade exists.
  • VolumeToday's volume against the same time on the last 10 days. 2× or more means the stock is "in play" (Bellafiore, Aziz); breakouts work best then.
  • ResultsTradingView's own simulation of every DT Core trade on the days loaded on this chart, with one tick of slippage and no commissions. The full report is in the Strategy Tester on the desktop site.

What it does, in order

The rules behind the signals

  1. Mark the opening range. The high and low of the first minutes after the open. Toby Crabel showed in 1990 that breakouts from it carry follow-through; Andrew Aziz teaches the same trade for stocks.
  2. Wait for a candle to close outside it. With strong volume, and on the side of VWAP that agrees (Shannon: VWAP is the day's fair price).
  3. Size from the stop. Stop at the middle of the range; shares = dollars at risk ÷ distance to the stop (Tharp).
  4. Let the trade end itself. Target at 2× risk, stop, or 5 minutes before the close. At most one BUY and one SELL a day, none after the cutoff.
  • 0.5–1% per tradeElder's ceiling is 2%. Start small while you learn.
  • Daily stop at −3RThree full losses and you're done for the day.
  • Paper trade firstRun 20 or more signals on TradingView's Paper Trading account before using real money. Many days produce no signal; that's by design.
  • Check your broker's rulesUS margin accounts face pattern-day-trader requirements.

Earlier questions

I saved the script. Why is nothing on the chart?

Saving and adding are two separate steps, and you need both. Saving stores the code in your TradingView account. If it saved without a red error message, the code compiled fine. Adding puts it on a chart:

On the chart, tap ƒx Indicators → Personal (some versions say My scripts) → tap DT Core. Its name appears in the top-left corner of the chart.

Added but still blank?

  • Daily chart. DT Core only draws on intraday charts. Switch to 5 minutes; the panel says so if you forget.
  • Indicator limit. The free plan fits very few indicators per chart. Remove Volume or anything else you're not using (tap its name → remove).
  • Hidden. Tap DT Core's name at the top left and make sure the eye icon is on.
  • Before the open or on a weekend. You'll see earlier days' lines and signals; today's start at 9:30.

What do "0930-1000" and "0930-0935" mean?

They were time windows on a 24-hour clock in New York time, written start-end without colons. DT Core no longer asks for them: you pick Opening range length: Auto, 5, 15 or 30 minutes, and it measures from the opening bell on its own.

1130-133011:30 AM – 1:30 PMThe midday lull, now shaded automatically

The chart settings didn't match my app.

Menus differ between app versions, and most of those settings didn't affect the signals. DT Core handles the timezone inside the script. The only chart settings you need: Candles, the 5-minute timeframe, and ETH on if you want pre-market lines.

To reach chart settings for anything else, long-press the price numbers on the right edge of the chart, then tap Settings.

DT Momentum and DT Volume

Optional extras

DT Core's signals don't depend on either one. Your plan has plenty of indicator room, so it comes down to what helps you read the chart.

  • DT Volume: keepIts bright bars now light up at 1.5× average volume, the same test DT Core uses. A BUY or SELL candle always has a bright volume bar under it, so you can see why the signal fired. Paste the new version below over your saved copy.
  • DT Momentum: optionalDT Core doesn't use it. Keep it if the squeeze dots help you; otherwise tap its name on the chart → remove. You don't need to delete the saved script; it sits unused in Personal.
DT Momentum · squeeze, 3/10 or RSI in one pane

Pick one oscillator under Settings → Oscillator. Squeeze (Carter): red dots mean energy is building; the first green dot is the release. 3/10 (Raschke) and RSI (Wilder) are the other choices.
